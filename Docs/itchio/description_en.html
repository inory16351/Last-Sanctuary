You, then — before the last sanctuary is swallowed by twilight completely — step forward.

Overview

The last sanctuary, with its light gone out. If the Nexus at its centre falls, that is the end.
You never control your companions directly. You only decide who to summon, where they stand, and how they fight.
Their minds are corrupted while they fight, and once corruption reaches its limit they stop listening to orders.
A fallen companion does not come back — and every upgrade and relic they carried is lost with them.

Core systems

Tactics — Attack type, battle line, retreat threshold. How each ally fights is decided before the fighting starts.

Random growth — Who arrives and which stats rise are both random. Only Hero Awakening and relics push a stat past the cap of 100.

Corruption & Mental Errors — Fighting eats away at the mind. At 100, one of eleven Mental Errors strikes at random.

The loop of a run

Prep — No enemies on the field. Summon allies, upgrade them, and re-set squads and rally points.

March & Battle — Fail to end the wave inside the timer and Enrage begins. The survivors grow stronger by the second.

Expand — Hunt the beasts outside the sanctuary for Energy and dig up what lies buried. What you earn is what makes the next wave survivable.

Also in the game

  • Squads & rally points — Group allies so they move and hunt together, then plant a flag to give that squad a position to hold.
  • Relics — Dig up the orange exclamation marks, and take what powerful foes and Epic beasts leave behind. Common, Rare, Epic.
  • Epic hunts — Rich rewards, but engaging one unprepared can wipe a squad. Kill it and it rises again later, fiercer each time.
  • Events — Choices open up between waves. Good and bad outcomes both land immediately, and cannot be undone.
  • Time control — 1× · 2× · 4× · 8×. You can still open windows and change orders while paused.
  • Continue — Saves automatically on upgrades, deaths, and cleared waves. There is only one slot.
